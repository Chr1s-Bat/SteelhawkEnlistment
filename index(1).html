❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Who can enlist?

Applicants aged 17 to 40 who submit a valid ID, contact details and accept the Code of Conduct.

What happens after I submit?

You receive a generated service number. A recruiter contacts you through your email and phone within 5 working days.

Can I change my branch later?

Yes. Branch transfer requests are allowed during Level 1 training, before final assignment.

Is my password stored?

This is a classroom demo. Nothing leaves your browser and no data is saved on a server.
